Privacy Policy

Gonia: Goniometer & Posture · Effective 20 November 2026

SupportTerms of Use
The short version: Gonia has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Patient records, measurements and photos you create stay on your iPhone. The App Store privacy label for Gonia is “Data Not Collected.”

1. Data we collect

None from the app. Gonia does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, health data or usage data. It contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, so App Tracking Transparency is not used.

2. What you create stays on your iPhone

Patients (identified by an alias you choose, never a required full name), visits, range-of-motion measurements, notes, posture photos, landmarks and settings are stored only in Gonia's private container on your device, with iOS Complete file protection: the files can't be read while the iPhone is locked.

Gonia's records are excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so they never leave the device through a backup. This also means that deleting the app, or erasing or losing the iPhone, permanently deletes the records. Keep your clinic's own chart as the record of care.

3. Camera and posture photos

Gonia uses the camera only when you take a posture photo. The photo is processed on-device by Apple's Vision framework to suggest anatomical landmarks. Photos are never uploaded, never sent to any server and never saved to your photo library; they are stored inside Gonia only if you save the capture, and deleting the capture or the patient deletes them. You can deny camera access and still use every measuring feature.

4. Motion sensors

Joint angles are computed on-device from your iPhone's accelerometer and gyroscope while a measurement is running. Sensor data is not stored beyond the angles you save.

5. Copying into your notes

When you tap Copy, Gonia places the note line on this device's clipboard only (it is not shared with your other devices through Universal Clipboard). Where you paste it is up to you.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple sends us anonymous subscription event records (for example: product, start, free trial, renewal or cancellation date and storefront, keyed by a transaction number) through App Store Server Notifications, which we process with our subscription analytics provider, RevenueCat, Inc., to count trials and subscriptions. These records contain nothing you enter in Gonia and cannot be linked to your patients or measurements. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Health information

Because nothing you enter leaves your device through Gonia, we do not receive, store or process protected health information. You remain responsible for how you handle patient information under the laws that apply to your practice (for example HIPAA), including what you paste into other systems.

8. Children

Gonia is a professional tool for clinicians and students. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

9. Your choices

You can delete any patient, visit, measurement or photo in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request, but we are happy to answer questions.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

Gonia is published by Qidient Global Ltd. Questions about privacy: support@qidientlabs.com.
