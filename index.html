Intended use

Gonia is a software goniometer for iPhone. It uses the iPhone's motion sensors to measure and record joint range of motion, and the camera to measure and record posture angles from photographs. It is intended for use by licensed clinicians and by students under their supervision. Gonia does not diagnose, screen for or treat any condition and does not replace clinical judgment.

Measuring range of motion

  1. Home → Measure, pick the joint and motion, and read its setup card (patient position, placement, method).
  2. Strap-on: strap the iPhone to the moving segment with any armband, screen facing you. Handheld: hold it flat along the segment, like an inclinometer.
  3. With the limb at the start position, tap Zero. Move through the range; hold still at end range and Gonia captures the trial automatically, or tap Capture.
  4. Take up to three trials; Gonia shows the mean. Tap Done to see the result, copy the note line or save it to a patient.

Getting reliable readings

  • Strap the phone snugly; if Gonia shows Phone slipped, re-zero, return to the start position and tap Zero again.
  • Use the two-reading mode where the setup card asks for it (lumbar motions, supine knee flexion): place the phone as shown for each reading.
  • For motions that don't start at 0°, use Set start to record the start angle.
  • Turn Voice on to hear each value spoken while your hands are busy.

Posture photos

  • From a patient, start a posture capture and choose the anterior or lateral view. The shutter waits until the iPhone is level (roll and pitch within ±2°).
  • Landmarks are suggested automatically. Confirm each one, or drag it onto the anatomy using the magnifier. Craniovertebral angle and sagittal pelvic tilt use landmarks you place yourself; skin markers improve repeatability.
  • Get the patient's consent before photographing. Photos stay inside Gonia and are never saved to the photo library.

Patients and progress

  • Patients are identified by an alias (for example “J.D.”) and an optional chart number. Each visit keeps its measurements and posture captures.
  • Progress charts compare left and right and show the change since the initial evaluation.
  • Protocol templates (TKA post-op, ACL post-op, frozen shoulder, cervical, lumbar) list the motions to measure at a visit.

Your data

Everything is stored only on your iPhone, protected while the device is locked, and excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Deleting Gonia, or erasing or losing the iPhone, permanently deletes its records, so keep your clinic chart as the record of care. See the Privacy Policy.

Safety information

Follow the setup card for each motion and strap the iPhone snugly; re-zero when Gonia reports that the phone slipped. Automatically suggested posture landmarks carry about ±5–10° of uncertainty; confirm or adjust each landmark before relying on a value. Re-measure any reading that does not match your clinical examination. Stop a measurement if the patient reports pain. Obtain the patient's consent before taking posture photos. Records are stored only on the device and are excluded from backups; keep the clinic chart as the record of care. Report a problem with a reading to support@qidientlabs.com.
